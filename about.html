Our Story

The Velvet Rose is a Lahore-based boutique dedicated to the art of premium gifting, where we blend the natural beauty of exotic fresh flowers with the intricate charm of handcrafted crochet art. Our mission is to turn every celebration into an everlasting memory by ensuring each creation is meticulously curated with luxury, soul, and a touch of artisanal elegance. We strive to bring a unique, sophisticated experience to your doorstep, making your special moments truly unforgettable through our commitment to quality and handcrafted excellence.
